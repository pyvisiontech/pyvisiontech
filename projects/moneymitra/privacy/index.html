1. Introduction

Welcome to MoneyMitra ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your personal and financial data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application (the "App"). Keeping your financial life private is our top priority.

By downloading, accessing, or using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

To provide and improve our services, we collect the following types of information:

A. Personal Information

When you register for an account, we collect identifiable information such as:

  • Account Data: First name, last name, and email address.
  • Authentication Data: Encrypted passwords and authentication tokens (managed securely by our backend provider, Supabase).

B. Financial Information

As an expense tracking application, the App processes data related to your finances that you manually input:

  • Transaction Data: Amounts, categories, dates, descriptions, and notes related to your income and expenses.
  • Shared Finances: Information regarding shared expenses, group balances, and group members when utilizing the "Family Groups" feature.

C. Usage and Device Data

We may automatically collect device-specific information to ensure the App functions correctly, including:

  • Device model, operating system version, and unique device identifiers.
  • Crash reports and diagnostic data to help us troubleshoot errors.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • Providing the Service: To create and manage your account, store your transaction history, and facilitate group expense sharing.
  • Smart Categorization: To automatically analyze and categorize your transaction inputs accurately to provide budget insights.
  • Communication: To send you essential application updates, security alerts, and in-app notifications (such as group invitation alerts).
  • Security: To authenticate your login attempts and enforce Row Level Security (RLS) to ensure you can only access your own data.

4. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal or financial information to third parties. We only share information in the following limited circumstances:

  • With Service Providers: We use trusted third-party providers to host our infrastructure securely (e.g., Supabase for database hosting and authentication). These providers are bound by strict data processing agreements.
  • Within Groups: If you join or create a Family Group, your name and the shared expenses you post will be visible to other members you invite into that specific group.
  • For Legal Reasons: We may disclose your information if required to do so by law or in response to valid requests by public authorities.

5. Data Security

The security of your personal and financial data is critical to us. We implement robust, industry-standard security measures, including:

  • Row-Level Security (RLS): Database-level security to guarantee that your financial data can only be requested and viewed by your authenticated account.
  • Encryption: Data sent between the App and our servers is encrypted in transit using secure TLS/SSL protocols. Password hashes are encrypted at rest.

However, please remember that no method of transmission over the internet or cellular networks is 100% secure.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain your personal and financial data only for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide you with the App's services.

  • Your Right to Delete: You have the right to request the deletion of your account and all associated data at any time. You can delete your account directly through the App settings or by contacting us. Once deleted, your transaction history cannot be recovered.

7. Children's Privacy

Our App is not intended for use by children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. If we become aware that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we will take steps to delete such data from our servers immediately.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in legal requirements or our App's features. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Effective Date" at the top. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.
